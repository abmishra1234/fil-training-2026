# NovaPay Leave Policy (HR-POL-01)

NovaPay Technologies is a fictional fintech company used for training. This handbook is not a real policy.

## Earned leave
Every full-time employee accrues 2 days of earned leave per completed month, which is 24 days per calendar year. Earned leave is credited on the first working day of each month in the HRMS portal "PeopleDesk".

## Carry forward and encashment
Up to 10 unused earned-leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Days above 10 lapse on 31 December. Encashment of earned leave is allowed only at the time of exit, capped at 30 days, and is paid at the basic salary rate.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Graduate trainees and interns
Graduate trainees in their first six months accrue earned leave at 1.5 days per month. Interns get 1 day of leave per completed month and their leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Applying for leave
Leave must be applied in PeopleDesk at least 3 working days in advance, except sick leave. Your reporting manager approves it. If the manager does not act within 48 hours, the request is auto-escalated to the skip-level manager.
