# Learning and Certification (HR-POL-07)

## Annual learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 50,000 for courses, books and conferences. Graduate trainees get INR 25,000 in their first year. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Certifications
Cloud and security certifications (for example AWS, Azure, CKA, CISSP) are reimbursed fully on passing, including one re-attempt. The exam fee is reimbursed after the certificate is uploaded in PeopleDesk.

## Study leave
Employees can take up to 3 days of paid study leave per year for a certification exam.

## Retention clause
If an employee leaves within 12 months of a reimbursement above INR 40,000, the amount is recovered proportionately in the full and final settlement.

## Internal academy
The "NovaAcademy" portal hosts free internal courses on GenAI, secure coding, payments domain and communication skills. Completing the GenAI track is mandatory for graduate trainees in their first quarter.
