# FIL Domestic Travel Expense Policy (training extract, fictional)
Knowledge owner: Finance Policy team · Version 2026.2

## 1. Daily limits (INR)
| Grade | Meals per day | Hotel per night, tier-1 city | Hotel per night, tier-2 city | Local travel per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 600 | 4,000 | 3,000 | 1,500 |
| L2 | 900 | 6,000 | 4,500 | 1,500 |
| L3 | 1,200 | 8,000 | 6,000 | 2,500 |

Limits apply per calendar day per category (all meals on one day are added together).
Anything above the limit is not reimbursed; the rest of the item still is.

## 2. City tiers
Tier-1: Mumbai, Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida), Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune.
Every other city is tier-2.

## 3. Receipts and deadlines
* A receipt is required for any single item above INR 500. Without it the item is not reimbursed.
* Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Older items are not reimbursed.

## 4. Never reimbursable
Alcohol, traffic fines and penalties, personal entertainment (movies, games).

## 5. Outcomes
* APPROVE – everything claimed is reimbursable
* PARTIAL – some of it is reimbursable
* REJECT – nothing is reimbursable
* NEEDS_INFO – the claim cannot be checked (unknown employee, HR system unavailable, missing data)
