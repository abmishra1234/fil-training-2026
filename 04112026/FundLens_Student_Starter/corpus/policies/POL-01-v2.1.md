# Switching Policy

**Policy POL-01 | Version 2.1 | Effective 2025-01-01 | Status: SUPERSEDED**  
Owner: Head of Client Services. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

> **SUPERSEDED.** This version was replaced by POL-01-v3.0 on 2026-07-01. Do not use it to answer client questions. Kept for audit purposes only.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how requests to switch between Lumen Crest Funds SICAV sub-funds are processed and charged.

## 2. Processing

2.1 Switch instructions follow the dealing cut-off. Both legs of the switch are priced on the same dealing day.
2.2 The minimum switch amount is USD 500.

## 3. Switching fees

3.1 Clients may make up to 3 free switches in each calendar year.
3.2 The fourth and any later switch in a calendar year carries a switching fee of 0.50% of the amount switched.
3.3 Any servicing analyst may waive the switching fee at their discretion where the client is dissatisfied.

## 4. Frequent trading

4.1 More than 6 switches in a calendar year are referred to the Market Timing Review team.
