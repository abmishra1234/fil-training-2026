# Switching Policy

**Policy POL-01 | Version 3.0 | Effective 2026-07-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Client Services. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how requests to switch between Lumen Crest Funds SICAV sub-funds are processed and charged.
1.2 It applies to all retail share classes (A and Y). Institutional I share class switches follow the client's investment management agreement.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 A **switch** is a redemption from one LCI sub-fund and a subscription into another LCI sub-fund, both dealt on the same dealing day.
2.2 A **calendar month** runs from the first to the last calendar day of the month, based on the dealing date.

## 3. Processing

3.1 Switch instructions follow the dealing cut-off in POL-03 §3. Both legs of the switch are priced on the same dealing day.
3.2 The minimum switch amount is USD 1,000 (or EUR 1,000 for EUR share classes).
3.3 The switch must meet the minimum holding of the target share class. Switches into the I share class need an eligibility check by the Institutional Desk.
3.4 No entry charge is applied to the subscription leg of a switch. Only the switching fee in §4 may apply.

## 4. Switching fees and waivers

4.1 The first switch in any calendar month is free of charge.
4.2 The second and any later switch in the same calendar month carries a switching fee of up to 1.00% of the amount switched. The fee is paid into the receiving fund to protect remaining investors.
4.3 The fee is shown on the contract note sent to the client.
4.4 The switching fee may be waived only in two cases: (a) the switch corrects an error made by LCI, or (b) the client is registered as vulnerable under POL-05. A waiver needs Team Lead approval recorded in the CRM before the switch is dealt.
4.5 Servicing analysts must not promise a waiver to a client before approval under §4.4 is recorded.

## 5. Frequent trading

5.1 More than 4 switches in any rolling 30-day period are referred to the Market Timing Review team.
5.2 LCI may refuse or delay a switch that appears to be part of a short-term trading pattern that could harm other investors.

## 6. Communication to clients

6.1 When a client asks about fees for a switch, state the rule in §4.1 and §4.2 and the date the policy became effective.
6.2 Do not describe a switch as "free" if it would be the second or later switch in that calendar month.

## 7. Internal note (INTERNAL ONLY - do not quote to clients)

7.1 Team Leads may approve up to 3 switching-fee waivers per calendar month without Head of Client Services sign-off. Further waivers need Head of Client Services approval.
7.2 Waiver codes for the CRM: WV-ERR (LCI error), WV-VUL (vulnerable client).
