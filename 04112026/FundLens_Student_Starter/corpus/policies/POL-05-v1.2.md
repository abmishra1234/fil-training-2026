# Vulnerable Clients Policy

**Policy POL-05 | Version 1.2 | Effective 2026-02-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Vulnerable Client Champion. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Some clients may be more likely to suffer harm, for example because of bereavement, serious illness, financial difficulty, low confidence with financial matters, or a major life event.

## 2. Recognising vulnerability

2.1 Signs include: mention of a recent death, illness or hospital stay; confusion about earlier instructions; repeated requests for help; mention of money worries.
2.2 Do not ask for medical details. Record only what is needed to support the client, and only with the client's consent.

## 3. Actions

3.1 Offer more time, a call-back, or communication in another format.
3.2 Do not use pressure or urgency in any message.
3.3 With consent, register the client as vulnerable in the CRM (flag VUL) and inform the Vulnerable Client Champion.
3.4 A registered vulnerable client may be eligible for a switching-fee waiver under POL-01 §4.4.

## 4. Language

4.1 Use short sentences, plain words and a warm, respectful tone. Avoid jargon or explain it.
