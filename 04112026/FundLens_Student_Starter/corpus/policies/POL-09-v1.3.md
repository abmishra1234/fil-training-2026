# Account Changes and Verification Policy

**Policy POL-09 | Version 1.3 | Effective 2026-02-15 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Client Services. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Change of address and contact details

1.1 Address changes are accepted through the secure client portal or a signed change form.
1.2 Email address changes need confirmation from the old email address or a verified phone call.

## 2. Change of name

2.1 Name changes need a certified copy of the supporting document (for example a marriage certificate).

## 3. Change of bank details

3.1 Bank detail changes are never accepted by plain email or chat, because these are a common fraud route.
3.2 They are accepted only through the secure portal or a signed form, followed by a call-back to the phone number already on file.
3.3 After a change, redemption payments to the new account are held for 5 business days.
3.4 If a request looks suspicious (urgent tone, new email address, request to pay a third party), do not process it. Report it to the Fraud Team the same day.
