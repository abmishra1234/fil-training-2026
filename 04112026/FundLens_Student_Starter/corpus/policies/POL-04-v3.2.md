# Complaints Handling Policy

**Policy POL-04 | Version 3.2 | Effective 2026-04-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Complaints Manager. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. What counts as a complaint

1.1 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, oral or written, about LCI's service or products, whether justified or not.
1.2 If a client says they are unhappy, treat it as a complaint even if they do not use the word "complaint".

## 2. Timescales

2.1 Log the complaint in the CRM within 1 business day of receipt.
2.2 Send a written acknowledgement within 2 business days.
2.3 Send a final response within 8 weeks of receipt. If this is not possible, write to the client explaining why and when they can expect a response.
2.4 If the client is not satisfied with the final response, tell them they may refer the complaint to the Financial Services Ombudsman (fictional) within 6 months.

## 3. Handling standards

3.1 Acknowledge the client's concern in plain language and say what happens next.
3.2 Do not admit liability or offer compensation in writing before the investigation is complete.
3.3 Redress up to USD 500 may be approved by a Team Lead. Higher amounts need the Complaints Manager.
3.4 Complaints about investment performance alone are answered with factual performance information and the relevant commentary. Poor performance is not, by itself, a service failure.
