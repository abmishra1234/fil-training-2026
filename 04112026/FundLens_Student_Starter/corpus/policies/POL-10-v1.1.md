# Bereavement and Account Transfer Policy

**Policy POL-10 | Version 1.1 | Effective 2026-04-15 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Client Services. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Notification

1.1 When we are told that a client has died, express condolences and pass the case to the Bereavement Team within 1 business day.
1.2 The account is marked deceased. No new subscriptions, switches or regular investments are processed.

## 2. Documents needed

2.1 A copy of the death certificate.
2.2 Proof of the executor's or legal heir's authority (for example a grant of probate, succession certificate or letter of administration).
2.3 Identity documents for the executor or heir.

## 3. Timescales

3.1 The Bereavement Team contacts the family within 5 business days of the notification.
3.2 Once documents are verified, holdings are transferred or redeemed within 10 business days.

## 4. Tone

4.1 Use gentle, plain language. Do not ask for documents in the first reply beyond what is needed to start the process. Treat the contact as vulnerable under POL-05.
