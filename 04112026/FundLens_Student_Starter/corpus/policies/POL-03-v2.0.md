# Dealing Cut-off and Valuation Policy

**Policy POL-03 | Version 2.0 | Effective 2026-01-15 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Fund Operations. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy defines dealing days, cut-off times and how prices are calculated.

## 2. Dealing days

2.1 A dealing day is every day that banks are open for business in Luxembourg.
2.2 The funds do not deal on 1 January, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 24, 25 and 26 December, and 31 December.

## 3. Cut-off time

3.1 Orders received and accepted by **13:00 Central European Time (CET/CEST)** on a dealing day are dealt at that day's price.
3.2 Orders received after the cut-off are dealt on the next dealing day at that day's price.
3.3 For staff in India: 13:00 CET equals 17:30 IST in winter (late October to late March) and 13:00 CEST equals 16:30 IST in summer.
3.4 The time of receipt is the time the order reaches LCI's dealing system, not the time the client sent it.
3.5 A late order may be backdated only if the delay was caused by LCI. This needs Dealing Manager approval.

## 4. Valuation and prices

4.1 Each fund's net asset value (NAV) per share is calculated using closing market prices on the dealing day.
4.2 Prices are published by 09:00 CET on the next business day.
4.3 Orders are dealt on a forward-pricing basis: the client never knows the exact price at the time of placing the order.
