# Fees and Charges Policy

**Policy POL-02 | Version 1.4 | Effective 2026-03-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Fund Operations. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy explains the charges investors may pay and how Client Services must describe them.

## 2. Entry and exit charges

2.1 The maximum entry charge for each share class is stated in the fund master data and the KID. Distributors may charge less than the maximum.
2.2 No LCI sub-fund charges an exit fee.
2.3 Y and I share classes have no entry charge.

## 3. Ongoing charges (OCF)

3.1 The ongoing charges figure (OCF) is the annual cost of running the fund, expressed as a percentage of the fund's value. It is taken from the fund's assets and is already reflected in the share price and in published performance.
3.2 Always quote the OCF from the latest fund master data (DATA-FUND-MASTER) or the current KID, and state the share class and as-of date.
3.3 If a factsheet shows a different OCF from the fund master data, the fund master data is the authoritative source. Note the discrepancy to Fund Operations.

## 4. Transaction costs

4.1 Transaction costs are the costs of buying and selling the fund's investments. They are shown separately in the KID and are not included in the OCF.

## 5. Swing pricing

5.1 To protect existing investors, a sub-fund's price may be adjusted ("swung") by up to 2.00% on days with large net inflows or outflows.
5.2 Swing pricing is not a fee charged to the client and must not be described as one.

## 6. Fee errors and refunds

6.1 If LCI charged a client incorrectly, the difference is refunded within 10 business days of the error being confirmed, where the amount is USD 25 or more (or EUR 25).
6.2 Refunds below USD 25 are reinvested into the client's holding.

## 7. Rebates

7.1 Fee rebates to retail clients are not permitted.
