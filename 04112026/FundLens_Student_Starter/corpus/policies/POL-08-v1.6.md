# Redemption and Settlement Policy

**Policy POL-08 | Version 1.6 | Effective 2026-03-15 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Fund Operations. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Settlement

1.1 Redemption proceeds are paid within 3 business days after the dealing day (T+3) for all sub-funds except the LCI Euro Short Duration Bond Fund (F06), which settles at T+2.
1.2 Proceeds are paid only to a bank account in the client's own name that is registered on the account.
1.3 If payment is late because of LCI, interest at the fund's base-currency overnight rate is paid for each day of delay, where the amount is USD 25 or more.

## 2. Partial redemptions

2.1 After a partial redemption the remaining holding must be at least the share class minimum (USD 2,500 or EUR 2,500 for A and Y classes). Otherwise the whole holding is redeemed after the client is told.

## 3. Large redemptions

3.1 If total redemptions on one dealing day exceed 10% of a fund's value, the board may defer the excess to the next dealing day. This protects remaining investors.

## 4. New bank details

4.1 Proceeds cannot be paid to bank details changed in the last 5 business days. See POL-09 §3.
