# Data Protection and PII Handling Policy

**Policy POL-07 | Version 2.0 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Data Protection Officer. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Identity checks

1.1 Before discussing an account by phone, verify the caller with two of: account number, date of birth, registered postcode or PIN code.
1.2 Email replies may confirm general fund information without verification but must not reveal holdings, balances or transactions unless the email address matches the one on file.

## 2. Minimum data

2.1 Use only the personal data needed for the task.
2.2 Mask account numbers in all written communication, showing only the last 4 characters (for example ****4821).

## 3. AI tools

3.1 Client personal data may be sent to an AI model only through the approved LCI AI Gateway, which masks names, email addresses, phone numbers and account numbers before the model call.
3.2 Secrets (API keys, passwords, internal system URLs) must never be included in prompts, logs or documents.
3.3 Prompts and responses are logged with personal data masked and kept for 12 months for audit.

## 4. Breaches

4.1 If personal data is sent to the wrong person, report it to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.
