# Client Communications Standards

**Policy POL-06 | Version 2.3 | Effective 2026-05-01 | Status: ACTIVE**  
Owner: Head of Compliance. Applies to: Client Services (Gurugram, Bengaluru and Luxembourg servicing hubs).

## 1. Purpose

1.1 These standards apply to every message sent to a client, including replies drafted with AI tools. All AI-drafted replies must be reviewed and approved by a person before sending.

## 2. No personal advice

2.1 LCI Client Services provides information only. Staff must not tell a client what to buy, sell, hold or switch, or say that a fund is suitable for them.
2.2 When a client asks for advice, say that we cannot give personal advice and suggest they speak to a financial adviser. Then offer factual information.

## 3. No forecasts or guarantees

3.1 Do not predict future returns, prices or income, and do not describe any return or income as guaranteed, certain or risk free.
3.2 Portfolio manager views from commentaries may be shared only as views, clearly attributed, with the date of the commentary.

## 4. Quoting performance and charges

4.1 When quoting performance, always state the period, share class, currency and as-of date.
4.2 Any message that quotes past performance must include: "Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future returns."
4.3 Any message that quotes performance or risk figures must include the risk warning: "The value of investments and the income from them can go down as well as up and you may get back less than you invested."
4.4 When quoting charges, use the OCF from the fund master data and name the share class.

## 5. Tone and format

5.1 Use plain English, short paragraphs and a polite, professional tone. Greet the client by name and sign off as "Client Services Team, Lumen Crest Investments".
5.2 Do not include internal-only policy text, internal approval limits, CRM codes or staff names other than the portfolio manager named in public documents.
5.3 Reply to client emails within 2 business days.

## 6. Personal data

6.1 Never include another client's personal data. Show account numbers masked, with only the last 4 characters visible.
