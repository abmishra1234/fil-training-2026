## 2. Objective and investment policy

Aims to achieve capital growth over time by investing at least 70% of its assets in equities of companies that are headquartered in, or do most of their business in, China and that benefit from domestic consumer spending.
The fund is actively managed. The benchmark is used for performance comparison only and the manager may invest in securities not included in it.
