# Hybrid Working Arrangement (HR-POL-04)

## Office presence
NovaPay follows a hybrid model. Employees must be present in the office on at least 3 days per week. The remaining days may be worked from a home location within India, with the approval of the reporting manager.

## Anchor days
Tuesday and Thursday are team anchor days. Every team member is expected in the office on anchor days so that planning, design reviews and pairing sessions can happen in person. Friday is the most common remote day.

## New joiners
New joiners, including graduate trainees, must work from the office for their first 45 calendar days to complete induction, security training and buddy pairing. Remote days start only after the induction sign-off.

## Working from another city
Working from a different city for more than 10 consecutive working days needs HR approval through a "Temporary Location Change" request in PeopleDesk, because of tax and insurance rules.

## Home setup allowance
After induction, each employee can claim a one-time home-office allowance of INR 12,000 for a chair, monitor or headset. Bills must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.
