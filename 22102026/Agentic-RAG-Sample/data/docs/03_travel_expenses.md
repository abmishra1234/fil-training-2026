# Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement (FIN-POL-02)

## Booking
All business trips must be booked through the internal travel desk "TripNova" at least 7 days before travel. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than 5 hours. Business class needs VP approval.

## Per diem
A daily per diem of INR 2,000 covers meals and local conveyance for domestic business trips in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune). In non-metro cities the per diem is INR 1,500. International per diem is USD 60 per day.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at INR 7,500 per night in metro cities and INR 5,000 per night elsewhere. Stays must be booked through TripNova so that the corporate rate applies.

## Claims
Expense claims must be filed in the "ClaimEasy" module within 15 days of returning from the trip, with original bills or e-invoices attached. Claims filed after 45 days are rejected automatically. Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursed.

## Client entertainment
Client meals are reimbursable up to INR 3,000 per head, only when a client from an external organisation is present and the client name is recorded in the claim.
