# Aceranker.ai Expense Categories and Exceptions (FIN-PROC-CLAIM)

## Domestic travel versus relocation
Temporary business travel uses the travel reimbursement policy. A permanent office relocation requires an individually approved relocation package. Employees must not combine relocation invoices with daily travel allowances for the same dates.

## Office meals
Meals at the employee's normal office are covered by the office facilities programme where available. They are not a domestic business-trip per diem. A late meeting or an evening commute does not automatically create travel allowance eligibility.

## Team events
Team event spending requires a cost-centre owner's approval before booking. The organiser submits a consolidated event claim with the attendee list. A team dinner without an external client must not be filed as client entertainment.

## Client entertainment evidence
Client entertainment claims must identify the external organisation, attendees and business purpose. The client entertainment cap is separate from employee subsistence on a business trip. Avoid claiming the same meal under both categories.

## Hotel advance
An approved travel desk booking may be paid directly to the hotel. An employee must not claim the prepaid room amount again; only eligible incidental expenses supported by bills may be submitted after checkout.

## Personal extensions to business travel
Employees pay personal nights, meals and local transport outside the approved business itinerary. The travel desk records the business and personal dates separately. Personal extensions do not increase the approved business hotel cap.

## Claim correction
A returned claim may be corrected using the original claim reference and the finance review comments. Preserve the original travel return date when checking submission deadlines. Creating a new claim number does not restart the policy's time limit.

## Home equipment versus IT equipment
An employee-owned chair purchased under the home setup allowance is different from a company-issued monitor recorded as an IT asset. Attach the relevant approval and invoice to the correct expense category. Do not claim the same item under learning, travel and home equipment budgets.
