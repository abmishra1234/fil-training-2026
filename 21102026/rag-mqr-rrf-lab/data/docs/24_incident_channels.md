# Aceranker.ai Incident Channels and Follow-up (ENG-PROC-COMMS)

## Payment outages and unattended pages
When customer money movement is down for many users, treat it as Sev-1 and page the primary through PagerNova, day or night. Without acknowledgement in 10 minutes, the page escalates to the secondary on-call; after 20 minutes, contact the Engineering Manager on duty. A support ticket is not a substitute for an incident page. These timings follow ENG-PROC-05.

## Incident bridge
The appointed Incident Commander owns the bridge, timeline and action assignments. Responders state what they observed, what they changed and what happened next. Avoid multiple uncoordinated operators modifying the same payment recovery job.

## Primary coverage handover
The outgoing primary confirms pager ownership and open incidents with the incoming engineer. An unattended calendar entry is not a completed handover. The incident escalation matrix remains the authority when the assigned primary does not acknowledge a live page.

## Specialist assistance
The incident lead may request a database, cryptography or identity specialist after the initial engineering response. Specialist availability does not replace the primary-to-secondary escalation chain. Keep one accountable incident lead even when several teams assist.

## Customer communications
The communications owner prepares customer updates from verified incident facts and the current impact statement. Do not publish speculative causes or private identifiers. Engineering updates and customer messages have separate review responsibilities.

## Security incident routing
Suspected credential exposure is reported through the security channel as well as any affected service incident. Security coordinates containment and evidence handling. An engineering availability recovery alone does not close the credential exposure investigation.

## Incident review ownership
The service owner schedules the required review and assigns action owners. The review distinguishes detection gaps, mitigation decisions and preventive work. A meeting invitation without tracked actions does not complete the postmortem requirement.

## Allowance claim evidence
On-call allowance claims use the approved rotation roster and the covered dates. Ad hoc help in a team chat does not automatically count as a scheduled on-call shift. Finance checks the applicable weekday, weekend or holiday rate from the on-call policy.
