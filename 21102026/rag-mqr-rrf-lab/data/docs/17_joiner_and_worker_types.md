# Aceranker.ai Joining and Worker Categories (HR-PROC-JOIN)

## Employee policy scope
Full-time employees, graduate trainees, interns and vendor contractors are distinct worker categories. Benefits must be checked against the worker's category and applicable policy. A manager's project assignment does not change the category recorded in PeopleDesk.

## Graduate trainee buddy
Each graduate trainee is assigned a buddy for the first six months. The buddy helps with engineering tools, induction milestones and locating policy owners. Buddy support is separate from the incident on-call rotation and does not make the trainee an independent incident responder.

## Induction sign-off
The manager records completion of security training, equipment checks and buddy pairing in PeopleDesk. The hybrid working policy specifies the minimum office induction period. Completion of one online course does not by itself complete induction.

## Internship learning access
Interns may use the internal academy and team learning sessions during their engagement. External course purchases require a separate written project approval; the employee annual learning allowance is not automatically assigned to an intern.

## Vendor contractor equipment
Vendor contractors normally use vendor-managed equipment approved for corporate access. A project may issue a loan device with a recorded return date. A loan does not create eligibility for an employee home setup allowance or a permanent hardware allocation.

## Vendor contractor attendance
Vendor contractors follow the attendance schedule in their service agreement and project access plan. The employee hybrid arrangement does not amend a vendor agreement. The sponsor coordinates exceptions with procurement and security.

## Manager onboarding checklist
Managers confirm the reporting line, work location, repository groups and mandatory training assignments before the start date. Travel reservations, certification reimbursements and incident duties each require their own policy checks after onboarding.

## Worker category changes
When an intern or contractor becomes an employee, HR records an effective conversion date. Employee benefits begin according to the applicable policy from that date. Historical vendor expenses must not be claimed again under employee allowances.
