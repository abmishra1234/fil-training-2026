# Aceranker.ai Asset Lifecycle Procedures (IT-PROC-ASSET)

## Broken laptop
A hardware failure should be reported through HelpNova with the asset identifier and symptoms. IT arranges diagnosis and a temporary loan if needed. A broken device remains in asset inventory until IT records repair, replacement or disposal.

## Loan laptop
Loan laptops are temporary replacements with a recorded handover and return date. Copy only approved working files using managed storage. Receiving a loan does not restart the employee's standard device refresh cycle.

## Stolen device evidence handling
After a theft report, preserve the incident reference and submit supporting police documentation through the restricted IT ticket. Do not delay the initial notification while obtaining paperwork. IT controls wipe execution and account containment; the employee must not attempt to track a thief personally.

## Access card only loss
Report a missing access card so physical security can disable it. State whether any computing device was also lost. Physical security reviews building entry events while IT handles account risk when a device is involved.

## Personal phone separation
A personal phone used for corporate authentication must meet enrolment requirements. Removing corporate access does not transfer ownership of the phone to the company. Device-specific containment actions are decided by IT under the enrolment agreement.

## Monitor requests
Company monitors are allocated through HelpNova based on the approved role and stock. The assigned asset remains company property and must be returned at exit. A personal home setup reimbursement is a separate finance process.

## Asset return shipping
Remote employees arrange approved tracked shipping with IT before their final working day. Keep the receipt and packing record. The inventory team confirms receipt and condition before marking the return complete.

## Secure disposal
Only the authorised IT vendor may dispose of retired drives and laptops. IT records erasure evidence and the asset serial number. Employees must not sell, donate or retain an obsolete company laptop without formal asset release.
