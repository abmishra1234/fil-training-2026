# Aceranker.ai Engineering Glossary

- UTR: Unique Transaction Reference, the ID a bank assigns to a payment.
- CAB: Change Advisory Board, approves risky production changes.
- RTO: Recovery Time Objective, the maximum time a service may be down after a disaster.
- RPO: Recovery Point Objective, the maximum data loss measured in time.
- SLO: Service Level Objective, the internal reliability target for a service.
- T+1: one business day after the transaction day.
- HSM: Hardware Security Module, stores signing and encryption keys.
- KYC: Know Your Customer, identity checks done before onboarding a customer.
- Idempotency key: a client-generated key that makes retries safe by preventing duplicate processing.
- Ledgerline: Aceranker.ai's settlement and ledger service.
- Gatekeeper: Aceranker.ai's identity and token service.
