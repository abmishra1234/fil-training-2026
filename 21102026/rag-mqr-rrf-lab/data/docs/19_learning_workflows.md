# Aceranker.ai Learning Approval Workflows (HR-PROC-LEARN)

## Course purchase approval
Obtain manager approval before paying for a course from the annual learning budget. The request must state the provider, learning objective and expected cost. Approval reserves budget but does not change the worker category's annual entitlement.

## Certification result evidence
Upload the passing certificate and exam invoice to PeopleDesk before requesting reimbursement. Keep receipts for each eligible attempt. A course attendance badge is not evidence of passing a separate professional certification exam.

## Conference attendance
Conference registration may use the learning budget with manager approval. Associated business travel follows the travel policy and needs separate booking approval. A registration invoice must not include an unitemised personal holiday package.

## Study leave scheduling
Discuss exam dates with the manager before booking study leave. Record the leave category in PeopleDesk so it is not deducted from ordinary earned leave. The learning policy sets the annual number of paid study days.

## Internal academy completion
Internal academy courses record completion when the final assessment is passed. The GenAI track assignment for graduate trainees is monitored by the cohort coordinator. Completion of an optional cloud workshop does not satisfy a different mandatory track.

## Team subscriptions
A centrally purchased team subscription is owned by the sponsoring cost centre. Individuals must not claim its full price again as a personal course expense. Shared account credentials are prohibited even when a subscription supports multiple named seats.

## Learning reimbursement recovery review
HR and finance review the reimbursement date, eligible amount and employee exit date when a retention clause may apply. Employees can request an itemised calculation before final settlement. Managers cannot waive a recovery through an informal chat message.

## Books and accessibility
Books and accessible learning materials may be requested against an approved learning objective. Record individual receipts and avoid uploading payment card details. Accessibility assistance is coordinated privately with HR and does not require disclosure to the whole team.
